What is this?

A tattoo studio app that lets you try your designs on 3D body references. Choose a model and skin tone, then move, resize, rotate and mirror your tattoos. You can adjust body proportions on the MakeHuman model and try up to 16 tattoos in one study.

Tattoos follow the shape of the body. Tight curves and large designs can leave seams or gaps, so check a few angles and confirm the final fit with your artist.

How does it work?

The app runs in your browser, using WebGPU where available and WebGL2 as a fallback. Import a transparent PNG or WebP to get started.

Your artwork and studies stay in this browser. The app doesn’t upload them. Download a PNG placement reference for your artist, or a JSON workspace backup to keep your work and move it to another device.

Browser storage can be cleared or lost. Keep a separate backup. More about privacy and storage.
