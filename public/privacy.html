Privacy and storage

Your work stays in this browser

tattoo you stores your studies and imported artwork in this browser on this device, using IndexedDB. The app doesn’t upload your artwork or studies. There are no tattoo you accounts or automatic project sync.

Each browser and site address has its own workspace. The public website and a locally running copy keep separate workspaces.

Keep a separate backup

Clearing site data, private browsing, browser storage eviction or losing your device can remove your work. The app requests persistent storage when available, but your browser may refuse it or clear it later.

Download a JSON workspace backup to keep your studies and artwork together. To move your work, transfer that file and restore it in the destination browser. Restoring replaces that workspace; a safety backup download starts first. Check that you’ve kept the downloaded file.

PNG placement references and JSON backups are created in your browser and downloaded to your device. You choose whether to share them. A PNG reference shows a view of your study; keep a JSON backup if you want to continue editing.

If saving fails, keep the editor open and download a backup before reloading. Rendering and storage support depend on your browser and device.

Visiting the website

The hosting provider, Cloudflare, receives normal page and asset requests, including network information such as your IP address. The app’s models, fonts and scripts are served with the site. Application code includes no analytics or advertising trackers.
